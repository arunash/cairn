# Cairn — launch kit

## Viral LinkedIn post

> **Your brokerage is a black box.**
>
> It knows exactly what you own, what it cost you, what you've made, and what you'll owe the IRS in April. But try to actually *get* that out — sliced the way a real decision needs — and you hit a wall. Often it's hard. Sometimes it's genuinely impossible.
>
> And yet the same brokerage will happily sell you a wealth-management service.
>
> But before I hand anyone my money to manage, I want to answer the basics myself:
> → What is my portfolio actually *doing* right now?
> → How much have I collected from options and dividends?
> → What are my unrealized gains and losses?
> → Am I walking into a giant tax bill at year-end?
> → Where are the real opportunities — stock by stock?
>
> Almost no brokerage gives you those answers with the slicing and dicing real decisions require.
>
> Then Robinhood came along and democratized investing. And now, in the AI era, they've done something quietly radical: they've made their **agent (MCP) available to any user.** Your account — programmable, yours.
>
> Sit with that arc for a second: **from black boxes → to a truly AI-first brokerage that lets you look at your own data, figure things out, and act on it.**
>
> So I built **Cairn** — a shell on the Robinhood agent that runs **locally, via Claude Code, on your own account.**
>
> It starts with the obvious money stuff, finally unified on one page:
> • your portfolio value and what it's doing
> • your current tax situation — cost basis, holding period, realized long vs short-term gains
> • income you've collected from options and dividends
> • unrealized gains and losses
> • opportunities, stock by stock
>
> That part is table stakes. The part that's genuinely *priceless*: an AI partner that explains those concepts **on your own data**, walks you through scenarios *before* you act, and keeps a ledger of money coming in and out — **all outside your brokerage, on your machine.** Read-only. Your keys never leave.
>
> It's open-source. I'm sharing the whole thing so anyone can run it, fork it, and build on top.
>
> ⭐ github.com/arunash/cairn · walkthrough → arunash.com/cairn
>
> **If you had an AI partner sitting on your own portfolio data — what would you want it to tell you?** Drop your ideas below. The best ones, I'll build.
